### Scheme Scope Summary

1. **PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)**:
   - Central Sector Scheme providing income support of ₹6,000 per annum to all landholding farmer families having cultivable land, payable in three equal installments of ₹2,000 every 4 months directly into bank accounts via DBT (PFMS / Aadhaar-seeded).
   - Contains strict exclusion criteria (institutional landholders, former/present constitutional post holders, ministers, serving/retired government officers, income tax payees, professionals like doctors, engineers, lawyers, CAs).

2. **PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana)**:
   - Comprehensive crop insurance scheme offering financial support to farmers suffering crop loss/damage arising from non-preventable natural risks (prevented sowing, standing crop yield loss due to dry spells/floods/pests, post-harvest losses up to 14 days for crops dried in cut-and-spread condition, localized calamities like hailstorm/landslide/inundation).
   - Uniform low premium payable by farmers: maximum 2.0% for Kharif food and oilseed crops, 1.5% for Rabi crops, and 5.0% for annual commercial/horticultural crops.

3. **KCC (Kisan Credit Card) & MISS (Modified Interest Subvention Scheme)**:
   - Flexible, single-window credit mechanism meeting short-term credit requirements for cultivation of crops, post-harvest expenses, produce marketing loans, consumption requirements of farmer household, and working capital for maintenance of farm assets and activities allied to agriculture.
   - Provides interest subvention up to ₹3 lakh loan limit: baseline interest rate of 7% p.a., with 3% prompt repayment incentive (PRI), bringing the effective net interest rate down to 4% p.a. for prompt-repaying farmers.
